Community Guidelines

BIGULA is a place to share creativity, discover videos, and connect with others. These guidelines help keep our community respectful, safe, and enjoyable.

Effective date: 19 October 2026

1. Treat people with respect

Every user deserves to participate without harassment, threats, or targeted abuse. Disagreements are allowed, but they must not become intimidation or personal attacks.

  • Do not harass, threaten, stalk, or intimidate another person.
  • Do not encourage others to target or abuse a user.
  • Do not publish private information about another person without permission.
  • Do not impersonate another person or misrepresent your identity to deceive people.

2. Keep content safe and lawful

Do not upload, stream, share, or promote content that is illegal, exploits people, or creates a serious risk of harm.

  • No content that promotes violence against people or encourages serious physical harm.
  • No threats of violence or content intended to coordinate real-world harm.
  • No exploitation, abuse, or sexual content involving minors.
  • No human trafficking, exploitation, or promotion of abusive activity.
  • No instructions or encouragement for dangerous illegal acts that could seriously harm people.
  • No content that unlawfully exposes personal, financial, or confidential information.

3. No hateful conduct

Do not attack, dehumanize, threaten, or encourage discrimination or violence against people because of protected characteristics such as race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, sex, or other characteristics protected by applicable law.

Educational, documentary, or counterspeech content may be treated differently when its purpose and presentation do not promote hatred or abuse.

4. Sexual content and nudity

Do not post sexual exploitation, non-consensual intimate imagery, or sexual content involving minors. Do not share intimate images of another person without their consent.

Sexually explicit content and other restricted material may be removed or limited to comply with BIGULA rules, applicable law, and platform requirements. Do not use BIGULA to solicit sexual exploitation or abuse.

5. Protect children and vulnerable people

BIGULA does not permit content that sexually exploits, grooms, abuses, or endangers children. Do not attempt to contact minors for exploitative purposes or share material that puts a child at risk.

Report suspected child exploitation or serious danger using BIGULA's reporting features. Do not download, repost, or redistribute suspected abusive material.

6. Be authentic

  • Do not use fake accounts to scam, manipulate, or harass people.
  • Do not artificially inflate views, likes, followers, or other engagement.
  • Do not use bots, scripts, or deceptive methods to manipulate BIGULA features.
  • Do not mislead users into revealing passwords, authentication codes, or financial information.
  • Do not distribute malware, phishing links, or deceptive promotions.

7. Respect intellectual property

Only upload content that you created, have permission to use, or are otherwise legally entitled to share. Respect copyright, trademarks, and other intellectual property rights.

BIGULA may restrict or remove content following a valid copyright complaint or other intellectual property report. Repeated or serious infringement may lead to account restrictions or termination.

8. Spam, scams, and harmful links

Do not use BIGULA to spam users, run fraudulent schemes, distribute harmful links, impersonate trusted organizations, or promote deceptive financial offers.

Content or messages designed to steal account access, payment information, or personal details may be removed, and related accounts may be restricted.

9. Videos, live content, and status posts

The same guidelines apply to videos, live broadcasts, status posts, comments, profile content, and other material shared through BIGULA.

  • Do not livestream criminal activity, abuse, or serious threats of harm.
  • Do not post videos intended to expose or endanger another person.
  • Do not misrepresent dangerous events in a way that creates an immediate risk to others.
  • Make sure you have permission before recording or sharing private conversations or sensitive situations where consent is required.

10. Comments and interactions

Comments and interactions should contribute to a useful and respectful community. BIGULA may remove comments that contain harassment, threats, hate, scams, spam, or other prohibited content.

You may use available account controls to manage interactions. Do not use comments or repeated messages to evade another user's decision to block or avoid you.

11. Reporting content

If you encounter content or behavior that appears to violate these guidelines, use the report feature available in BIGULA or contact us through our Help page.

When submitting a report, provide accurate information and explain the concern. Do not knowingly submit false reports to harass another user or manipulate enforcement.

Reports may be reviewed and may result in content removal, warnings, restrictions, or other appropriate action. Submitting a report does not guarantee a particular outcome.

12. Blocking and personal safety

Use BIGULA's blocking and privacy features where available to limit unwanted interactions. If someone threatens you or makes you feel unsafe, consider preserving relevant evidence and contacting appropriate local authorities when there is an immediate risk.

Blocking a user does not necessarily remove content that has already been copied, shared, or saved outside BIGULA.

13. How BIGULA enforces these rules

BIGULA may review reported content and take action when content or behavior violates these guidelines, our Terms of Service, or applicable law.

Depending on the seriousness and frequency of the violation, action may include:

  • Removing content or restricting its visibility.
  • Issuing a warning or requesting changes.
  • Restricting features, live access, or monetization eligibility.
  • Temporarily suspending an account.
  • Terminating accounts for serious or repeated violations.
  • Preserving or disclosing information when legally required or necessary to address serious safety concerns.

BIGULA considers the nature of the violation, its severity, the context, and relevant history when determining appropriate action. Where available, users may contact BIGULA to appeal an enforcement decision.

14. Creator and monetization rules

Creators must follow these guidelines when participating in creator, verification, or monetization features. Fraudulent engagement, deceptive identity claims, copyright infringement, scams, and attempts to manipulate earnings may result in loss of eligibility or other account action.

Creator earnings and verification are subject to the applicable BIGULA feature requirements and terms.

16. Updates to these guidelines

BIGULA may update these guidelines as the community and service develop. Updated guidelines will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Continued use of BIGULA after an update is subject to the revised rules, subject to applicable law.
