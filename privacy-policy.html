BIGULA · PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to BIGULA. This Privacy Policy explains how BIGULA handles information when you use our website, mobile application, and related services.

Effective date: 19 October 2026

1. Introduction

BIGULA is a video-sharing and content-discovery platform that allows users to create profiles, upload and watch videos, interact with other users, and access supported creator features.

This Privacy Policy describes the information BIGULA may collect, how that information may be used and shared, how it is protected, and the choices available to you. By using BIGULA, you acknowledge this policy. Where applicable law requires consent for particular processing, BIGULA will seek that consent separately.

2. Information BIGULA collects

The information processed depends on the features you use and the information you choose to provide.

  • Account and sign-in information: account identifiers and information provided through your chosen sign-in method, which may include your name, email address, profile image, and authentication details.
  • Profile information: username, display name, avatar, biography, profile preferences, and privacy settings.
  • User content: videos, captions, comments, profile images, status posts, reports, and other material you submit.
  • Activity and interactions: likes, follows, comments, video views, notifications, and other actions needed to provide BIGULA features.
  • Creator information: creator profile details, monetization status, earnings records, verification submissions, and related information when you use those features.
  • Support and safety information: information included in support requests, content reports, appeals, or communications with BIGULA.
  • Technical information: information that may be generated through use of the service, such as app version, device or browser details, diagnostic records, security logs, and network information, where collected by the systems in use.

BIGULA does not intentionally request information that is unnecessary for the feature you choose to use. The specific information processed may change as features are added or improved.

3. Information from Google sign-in

If you sign in using Google, Google may provide BIGULA with information permitted by the sign-in permissions you approve, such as your name, email address, profile picture, and an account identifier. BIGULA uses relevant information to authenticate you, create or access your account, and support your profile.

Google handles information collected through its own services according to Google's privacy policy and applicable terms. You can review or manage third-party account permissions through the relevant provider's settings.

4. Videos, camera, microphone, and photos

When you choose to record, upload, or broadcast content, BIGULA may request access to your camera, microphone, or selected media on your device. Access is intended to support the feature you choose, such as recording a video, selecting an existing video, or using a live feature where available.

You can manage these permissions through your device settings. If you deny permission, the related feature may not work. BIGULA processes the content you submit to provide the service, display it according to its visibility settings, and enforce applicable rules.

Before uploading, make sure you have the necessary rights and that your content does not expose private information without permission.

5. How BIGULA uses information

BIGULA may use information to:

  • Create, authenticate, and maintain user accounts.
  • Display profiles, videos, comments, likes, follows, and other interactions.
  • Personalize content discovery and help organize the video feed.
  • Provide notifications and other requested features.
  • Maintain creator profiles, verification processes, and earnings features where available.
  • Respond to questions, complaints, reports, and appeals.
  • Detect spam, fraud, unauthorized access, harmful activity, and policy violations.
  • Protect users, enforce BIGULA's policies, and maintain service security.
  • Diagnose technical problems, maintain reliability, and improve the service.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations and resolve disputes.

6. Public content and account visibility

Content you publish publicly, including videos, comments, usernames, and public profile details, may be visible to other users. Other people may copy, record, or share public content outside BIGULA.

Where BIGULA provides private-account or content-visibility settings, those settings help control access within the service. They cannot guarantee that content already viewed, copied, or shared by another person will be removed from elsewhere.

Please think carefully before posting personal information or content that you do not want other people to see.

7. How BIGULA shares information

BIGULA may share relevant information in the following circumstances:

  • With other users: when you publish content, maintain a public profile, or interact through features visible to other users.
  • With infrastructure providers: where necessary to host the website, authenticate accounts, store content, operate the database, or deliver the service.
  • With service providers: where needed for technical support, security, diagnostics, payment processing, or other functions that BIGULA actually uses.
  • For safety and legal compliance: when reasonably necessary to investigate abuse, protect users or BIGULA, comply with applicable law, or respond to valid legal requests.
  • With your direction: when you choose to publish information or authorize a particular disclosure.
  • During a business transition: if BIGULA's service or assets are reorganized or transferred, subject to appropriate protections and applicable law.

BIGULA does not sell personal information as a business model. Information is shared only as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable law.

8. Hosting, databases, and storage

BIGULA uses online infrastructure to make its website and features available. Depending on the feature, this may include hosting, authentication, database, and file-storage services. Information may be processed by those providers to operate and secure the service.

Service providers may process information under their own terms and privacy policies. BIGULA takes reasonable steps to use appropriate services and protect information handled through them.

9. Cookies and local storage

The BIGULA website or app may use browser storage, cookies, or similar technologies to maintain sign-in state, remember preferences, and support essential functions. The technologies used depend on the version of the service and its integrations.

Clearing browser storage or disabling certain technologies may sign you out or cause some features and saved preferences to stop working.

10. Creator earnings and verification

If you use creator monetization or verification features, BIGULA may process the information required to assess eligibility, review submissions, maintain earnings records, detect abuse, and administer those features.

Where an external payment provider is used, that provider may separately collect and process information needed to make payments or meet financial and legal requirements. Do not submit payment or identity documents except through an official BIGULA feature or an authorized provider.

11. Data retention

BIGULA retains information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain account functionality, support creator records, enforce policies, protect security, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations.

Retention depends on the type of information and the reason it is held. Certain records, such as security logs, reports, and legally required financial records, may need to be retained for a limited period even after an account is deleted.

12. Account deletion and data removal

You may request deletion of your BIGULA account through the account-deletion feature available on our website or in the app, where provided. You can also visit the following page:

BIGULA Account Deletion

After a valid request is verified, BIGULA will take appropriate steps to delete the account and associated personal information, subject to legal retention obligations, security requirements, dispute resolution, and other lawful exceptions.

Some information may remain temporarily in backups before it is overwritten or removed. Information that must be retained for legal, financial, or security purposes will be retained only as permitted or required. Content already copied or shared by other users may remain outside BIGULA's control.

Uninstalling the app from your device does not delete your BIGULA account or information stored on our systems.

13. Security

BIGULA takes reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, loss, alteration, and misuse. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

You can help protect your account by securing your sign-in method, keeping your device protected, and notifying BIGULA if you suspect unauthorized access.

14. International data processing

BIGULA and its service providers may process or store information in countries other than the country where you live. Data protection laws may differ between countries. Where required by applicable law, appropriate safeguards will be used for international transfers.

15. Children's privacy

BIGULA is not intended for children who are below the minimum age permitted by applicable law or the age requirements that apply to the service. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information where such collection is prohibited.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to BIGULA inappropriately, please contact us so the concern can be reviewed and appropriate action taken.

16. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing.

You can manage available profile and privacy settings, control device permissions, choose what content you publish, and request account deletion. To make a privacy request, contact BIGULA through the support options available on our website.

BIGULA may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request. Some requests may be limited where the law permits or requires us to retain certain information.

17. Third-party services and links

BIGULA may link to third-party websites or use third-party services. Those services may have separate privacy policies and terms. BIGULA is not responsible for the independent privacy practices of services it does not control.

18. Changes to this Privacy Policy

BIGULA may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the service, data practices, or legal requirements. The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date. Where required, BIGULA will provide additional notice or obtain consent.
