Account & login

Help with signing in and accessing your BIGULA account.

Back to SettingsReport a problem

Sign in with Google

Open Signup and follow the Google sign-in prompts. Return to BIGULA after the account flow completes.

If sign-in appears stuck

Refresh the page once. If the issue continues, close and reopen BIGULA and try again.

Use the correct account

If multiple Google accounts are on your device, confirm that you selected the account you want to use.

Keep your account secure

Do not share sign-in links or verification details with anyone. Use only the official BIGULA app or website.

Still unable to sign in?

Open Report a problem and describe the steps that lead to the issue.
