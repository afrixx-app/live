Using BIGULA

Learn the basics of finding videos, exploring creators and interacting with the BIGULA community.

Back to SettingsReport a problem

Explore your feed

Scroll through videos and use the feed tabs to discover content. Use Search to find videos or creators.

Open a creator profile

Select a creator’s name or profile image to view their profile and available videos.

Like and comment

Use the video actions to like a video or join the conversation in its comments. Sign in if prompted.

Follow creators

Open a creator profile and choose Follow. If an account is private, its follow rules may limit new followers.

Manage your interests

Open Settings → Interests, select topics you enjoy, and your selections are remembered on this device.
