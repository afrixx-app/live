Uploading videos

A quick guide to creating and managing videos on BIGULA.

Back to SettingsReport a problem

Start an upload

Open Create or Upload and choose a video from your device. Sign in if prompted.

Review your video

Follow the upload steps and check the title, description and available editing options before publishing.

Connection and upload issues

Keep the app open when possible and use a stable connection. If an upload fails, retry after checking your connection.

Manage existing videos

Open your account or creator area and select a video to view its available management options.

Safety and rights

Only upload content you have the right to share and follow BIGULA’s content rules.
